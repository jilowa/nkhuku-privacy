Privacy Policy

Nkhuku Manager · Last updated: 5 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Redmond Business Solutions Limited (“Redmond,” “we,” “us”) handles your personal information when you use the Nkhuku Manager mobile application (“the App”). We are committed to handling your data responsibly and in line with Zambia’s Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021.

1. Who we are

Redmond Business Solutions Limited is the data controller for the App. We are based in Lusaka, Zambia. If you have questions about this policy, your data, or your rights, contact us at william@redmond.co.zm.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the App. Specifically:

When you create a farm account:

  • Your name
  • Your farm name
  • Your mobile phone number (used to verify your account via SMS)
  • Your location, if you choose to provide it (optional)

When you use the App to manage flocks:

  • Flock details (breed, batch size, start date)
  • Daily entries you record (mortality, feed usage, weights, water intake, vaccinations, notes)

Automatically collected technical data:

  • App version, device type, operating system version
  • Crash reports and basic usage statistics (which screens are used and how often)
  • This technical data is collected through Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google

We do not collect:

  • Your contacts, photos, calendar, messages, or data from other apps
  • Your precise GPS location (unless you choose to enter a location yourself)
  • Payment or financial information (the App is free)
  • Government-issued identity numbers

3. Why we collect this information

  • Phone number — to create your account, verify it by SMS, and let you sign in on a new phone
  • Name and farm name — to personalize the App and identify your farm
  • Flock data — this is the App’s core function: helping you track your birds. This data belongs to you.
  • Optional location — to provide region-relevant insights in future versions
  • Technical and usage data — to fix bugs, improve performance, and understand which features matter most to farmers

Our legal basis for processing your data is your consent (given when you sign up) and the performance of our service to you (operating the App).

4. Who we share data with

We share your data only with the service providers we use to run the App:

  • Google Firebase (operated by Google LLC, USA) — used for authentication, SMS verification, the database that stores your flock data, and analytics. Google’s handling of this data is governed by their privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

We do not:

  • Sell your data to anyone
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Share your data with other farmers or third parties without your explicit consent
  • Disclose your data to any party except where required by Zambian law (for example, a valid court order)

5. International data transfers

Your data is stored on Google Cloud servers, which may be located outside Zambia. By using the App, you consent to this transfer, as permitted under Section 70 of the Data Protection Act. Google maintains industry-standard security including encryption of data in transit and at rest, and operates under recognised international data-protection frameworks.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you stop using the App, we keep your data for up to 12 months in case you return, and then it may be deleted. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us; we will action verified requests within 30 days.

7. Your rights under the Data Protection Act 2021

As a data subject in Zambia, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is being processed (this policy)
  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected (rectification)
  • Have your data deleted (erasure / “right to be forgotten”)
  • Object to certain processing of your data
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Receive your data in a structured, commonly-used format (data portability)
  • Not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Zambia

To exercise any of these rights, email william@redmond.co.zm. We respond within 30 days. We may ask you to verify your identity (typically by confirming your registered phone number) before acting on a request.

8. Security

We protect your data using:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) between the App and our servers
  • Encrypted storage on Google Cloud
  • Phone-number-based authentication, so only you can sign in to your account
  • Access controls limiting who at Redmond can view stored data

No system is completely secure. If we become aware of a data breach affecting you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours, as required by law.

9. Children

Nkhuku Manager is intended for poultry farmers aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe we have collected data from a minor, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you through the App or by email. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the current version. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
